About This Event

Join us for the Atlanta Women in AI x Thrive Nexus September Community Mingle! This collaborative event brings together two dynamic communities focused on innovation, technology, and professional growth. Whether you're actively working with AI or exploring how to integrate it into your work, this is the perfect opportunity to connect with like-minded professionals in a relaxed, supportive environment.

Event Agenda

6:00 PM - 6:15 PM

Check-in & Mingling

Check in, grab an appetizer, and start mingling. Have your QR code ready (LinkedIn or company profile), either printed or on your phone. Light networking as guests arrive. Stretch those social muscles.

6:15 PM - 6:30 PM

Icebreaker: "Scan & Stand"

Each guest will:

  • Stand and display their QR code
  • Introduce themselves with Name, Role & Company, how they're currently using AI — or how they'd like to be using it
  • Bonus: Share the worst use of AI you've seen lately (for a laugh or a warning)

Let's keep it lively, insightful, and just a touch ridiculous - like all great innovation.

6:30 PM - 6:50 PM

Group Connection Exercise

Prompt: "In a world driven by digital tools, what does meaningful connection look like to you?"

  • Pair off or break into groups of 3
  • Discuss for 5–7 minutes
  • A few brave souls can share highlights with the full group (totally optional, no pressure—we're not in a TED Talk)

Additional Questions for Discussion:

  • What was the last networking event you attended?
  • Was it virtual or in person?
  • What made it memorable—or what made you wish you'd stayed home?
6:50 PM - 7:30 PM

Open Networking: Mingle & Merge

This is where the real magic happens.

  • Mingle with members of the other organization
  • Conversation corners available by interest
  • Goal: Leave with 4–5 real connections—people who you want to get to know better professionally or personally. This isn't speed dating.
7:30 PM - 8:00 PM

Closing Cheers

  • Brief thank-you remarks and shout-outs to organizers and volunteers
  • Snap a group photo for LinkedIn—because if it's not posted, did it even happen?
  • Final mingling and informal wrap-up
