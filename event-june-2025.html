What Happens When AI Breaks™ PAST EVENT

Building Resilient Systems, Leading with Intent

May 27, 2025

Join us at a special evening meeting as Trina Hill talks with us about one of the major reasons women need to lead AI - women know how to look ahead. And, when things fail, women are resilient leaders who know how to rebuild - and rebuild better.

"We don't build trust in systems just because they work - we build trust in how we prepare for when they don't work. As AI continues to shape every part of our lives, real innovation means planning for failure, disruption, and recovery.

That's not fear. That's foresight.

And that's how women lead: with care, with clarity, and with the kind of resilience that doesn't just survive the future; it shapes it."

- Trina Hill

About This Event

In this thought-provoking session, Trina Hill explored the critical importance of building resilient AI systems and the unique perspective women bring to AI leadership. The discussion covered:

  • Planning for AI system failures and disruptions
  • Building trust through transparency and preparedness
  • The importance of foresight in AI development
  • How women's leadership style contributes to more resilient AI systems
  • Strategies for recovery and rebuilding when AI systems fail

Key Takeaways

This event highlighted the critical role women play in shaping the future of AI by bringing unique perspectives on resilience, foresight, and leadership. Attendees gained insights into:

  • Why planning for failure is essential in AI development
  • How to build trust in AI systems through transparency
  • The importance of diverse perspectives in creating resilient technology
  • Practical strategies for leading with intent in the AI space
