Who We Are

Atlanta Women in AI is a vibrant community of tech professionals, consultants, and marketers united by a shared vision: to amplify women's voices in artificial intelligence.

Our Mission

To create a supportive ecosystem where women in AI can connect, learn, and lead. We foster professional growth through knowledge sharing, mentorship, and collaborative innovation.
