Hands-on Session: Custom Use Cases for Custom GPTs

October 15, 2025 | Workshop

Learn to build custom GPTs that make your ChatGPT sessions more productive!

Join us for a hands-on opportunity to explore custom use cases for Custom GPTs. Learn more about building custom GPTs that will make your ChatGPT sessions more productive and less frustrating.

What You'll Learn

Follow along as we examine use cases, build a custom GPT for one of them, and then turn to building your own custom GPTs.

You'll walk out with a clearer understanding of how to build custom GPTs and how to deploy them in your chats to deliver a more streamlined and targeted end result.

About This Event

A subscription for Chat GPT PLUS ($20/month, cancel anytime) is required for the hands-on portion of our meetup.

Note: Custom GPTs are a subset of AI Assistants.
