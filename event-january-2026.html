Disarming Imposter Syndrome

January 21, 2026 | Workshop

Reconnect with the Truth of Your Experiences

We'll start 2026 by standing determinedly on the foundation we've already built and focusing on strengthening it even more.

Join us for a powerful session led by Becky Berry, where we'll explore the real roots of imposter syndrome and learn how to disarm it by reconnecting with the truth of our experiences.

What to Expect

Expect a lively, affirming conversation packed with tools to reinforce your leadership from the inside out. You'll find yourself creating a personal achievement mass that keeps you steady in the face of self-doubt and outside noise.

You'll Walk Away With

  • ✓ New language to reframe self-doubt
  • ✓ Practical reflection tools
  • ✓ A stronger connection to your own agency and unique brilliance

About the Speaker

Becky Berry is the cofounder, with Sue Yeh Johnson, of Atlanta Women in AI. She's a coach for progressives weary of being marginalized and determined to lead on their own terms - clearly, unapologetically, and with unshakable confidence.
