leadText: >-
  Most of us use AI in a chat window — ask a question, get an answer, then do
  something with it ourselves. Cowork closes that last gap: it works with your
  real files, runs on a schedule, and uses the tools you already have to produce
  the finished thing, not just the answer.


  **Alissa Condra** will walk through four automations she runs in her own business — from a simple bank-statement-to-spreadsheet task up to an interactive customer journey map built from folders of unstructured transcripts and proposals. Along the way she'll share the practical test she uses to decide what's worth building in Cowork versus what's fine to leave in a chat, and where the tool breaks down. Bring your own Cowork examples or questions and use cases and we'll save time for a group discussion.

  research, and she writes about what she's learning as she goes.
