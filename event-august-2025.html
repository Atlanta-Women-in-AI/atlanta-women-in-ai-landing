Stop Prompting and Start Building CustomGPTs

July 28, 2025 | Meeting

No coding. No overwhelm. Just the easiest way to create your own intelligent AI assistant.

One that remembers your voice, follows your style, and actually helps you get things done.

Are you retyping the same instructions over and over? Trying to make ChatGPT "remember" how you like things? Let's level up with CustomGPTs!

In this hands-on session, you'll learn how to maximize your ChatGPT sessions with reusable, personalized AI assistants.

We'll cover what makes a Custom GPT different from regular chats or Projects, walk through a real example, and then help you create one to bring your own ideas to life!

Whether you want to optimize your LinkedIn, plan a trip, or get career advice, you'll walk away with a working tool that saves time and actually works the way you think. No code. No tech background required. Just one of the easiest - and most powerful - ways to start building with AI.

Bring your laptop and your own custom GPTs to share with the group.

About Our Speaker

Lillāna Shah is a systems-minded founder, builder, and AI consultant dedicated to designing tools for the invisible work of modern life. She leads Late Summer Group (www.latesummergroup.com), where she develops FamTech apps like Poolerz and OTW, and advises business owners on how to design repeatable, privacy-conscious systems with AI.

What to Expect

  • Hands-on exercises to create your own AI Assistants
  • Real-world examples: self-care, organization, vacations, focus, and project planning
  • Bring your laptop and your curiosity for a lively, supportive session!
