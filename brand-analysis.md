## Updated Color Palette (Based on Logo)

After analyzing the Atlanta Women in AI logo, the color palette has been updated to match the brand identity:

### Primary Colors
- **Primary Teal**: #4ECDC4 (Main brand color from logo)
- **Dark Teal**: #2FA29A (Deeper teal accent)
- **Navy Blue**: #2C3E50 (Primary text and headers)
- **Dark Navy**: #1A252F (Footer and dark sections)

### Secondary Colors
- **Light Teal**: #7DD3D0 (Lighter accents)
- **Medium Teal**: #5EEAD4 (Hover states)
- **Gray Blue**: #3E4C59 (Secondary elements)

### Neutral Colors
- **White**: #FFFFFF (Primary background)
- **Off-White**: #F8FFFE (Subtle teal-tinted white)
- **Gray**: #64748B (Secondary text)
- **Light Gray**: #E8F4F3 (Teal-tinted gray for backgrounds)

### Gradient Combinations
1. Navy to Teal: #2C3E50 → #4ECDC4
2. Dark Navy to Dark Teal: #1A252F → #2FA29A
3. Teal to Light Teal: #4ECDC4 → #7DD3D0

These colors create:
- Professional yet vibrant appearance
- Strong brand consistency with logo
- Modern tech-forward aesthetic
- Accessible contrast ratios
