# Information Architecture & User Journey

## Single-Page Layout Structure

### 1. Hero Section (100vh)
- **Dynamic animated background** with gradient mesh
- **Bold headline**: "Amplifying Women's Voices in AI"
- **Subheadline**: Mission statement with animated text reveal
- **Primary CTA**: "Join Our Community" (smooth scroll to membership)
- **Secondary CTA**: "Next Event" (smooth scroll to events)
- **Visual elements**: 
  - Floating AI-themed icons with parallax
  - Particle effects or neural network animation
  - Video background option

### 2. About Us (Who We Are)
- **Split layout**: Text + Interactive graphic
- **Key stats**: Animated counters (130+ LinkedIn, 80+ members, etc.)
- **Core values**: Cards with hover effects
- **Mission visualization**: Interactive timeline or process flow

### 3. Why Join Us (Merged Initiatives Content)
- **Interactive card grid** showcasing benefits:
  - Monthly Meetings
  - Masterclasses
  - Think Tank Sessions
  - Networking Opportunities
  - 411 Connect Sessions
  - Mastermind Groups
- **Each card**: Expandable with micro-interactions
- **Visual hierarchy**: Most popular initiatives highlighted

### 4. Upcoming Events
- **Event carousel** with glassmorphism cards
- **Quick registration**: Inline forms
- **Calendar integration**: Visual monthly view
- **Event countdown timer** for next meeting

### 5. Success Stories / Testimonials
- **Rotating testimonials** with member photos
- **Achievement highlights**: Member wins
- **Before/after career journeys**

### 6. Get Involved (CTA Section)
- **Three pathways**:
  1. Join WhatsApp Community
  2. Attend Next Meeting
  3. Collaborate on Events
- **Interactive selection process**
- **Immediate next steps**

### 7. Footer
- **Newsletter signup** with animated input
- **Social links** with hover effects
- **Quick links** navigation
- **Contact information**

## User Journey Flow

1. **Arrival**: Captivating hero grabs attention (3-second rule)
2. **Interest**: Learn about the organization
3. **Desire**: See benefits and success stories
4. **Action**: Multiple clear pathways to engage

## Key Conversion Points
- Hero CTA → Membership section
- Initiative cards → WhatsApp signup
- Event cards → Registration
- Footer → Newsletter subscription

## Navigation Strategy
- **Sticky nav bar** with progress indicator
- **Smooth scroll** between sections
- **Section transitions** with fade/slide effects
- **Mobile**: Hamburger menu with full-screen overlay
