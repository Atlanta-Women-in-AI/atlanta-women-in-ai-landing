Who We Are

Atlanta Women in AI is a vibrant community of entrepreneurs, tech professionals, engineers, consultants, marketers, and more united by a shared vision: to amplify women's voices in artificial intelligence.

Our Mission

To create a supportive ecosystem where women in AI can connect, learn, and lead. We foster professional growth through knowledge sharing, mentorship, and collaborative innovation.

Past Events

A look back at our inspiring gatherings

JUN 19 2025

What Happens When AI Breaks™

📍 Improving, Alpharetta ⏰ 6:00 - 7:30 PM 👥 Evening Session

Building Resilient Systems, Leading with Intent

Trina Hill led an inspiring discussion on why women need to lead AI - because women know how to look ahead. When things fail, women are resilient leaders who know how to rebuild better.

"We don't build trust in systems just because they work - we build trust in how we prepare for when they don't work." - Trina Hill
Sponsors: Turnberry Solutions, Becky Berry Career Coaching, Yeh & Johnson Technology Consulting
MAY 21 2024

Harnessing AI IRL

📍 Improving, Alpharetta ⏰ 12:00 - 1:30 PM 💻 Hands-on Workshop

A hands-on session where members learned tips & tricks for using AI in real life. Participants shared their own AI hacks and discoveries.

Facilitators: Erika Robinson and Tiffany Whitener

Members brought their laptops for an interactive learning experience focused on using AI to organize, focus, and enjoy life.

Sponsors: Becky Berry Career Coaching, Johnson & Yeh AI & Tech Consulting, Turnberry Solutions
APR 16 2025

AI Prompts Party!

📍 Improving, Alpharetta ⏰ 12:00 - 1:30 PM 🎉 Interactive Workshop

Atlanta Women in AI's first prompt party! A hands-on event where facilitators showed the ins and outs of creating better AI prompts.

Facilitators: Sue Yeh Johnson and Becky Berry

Participants brought their laptops for an interactive session exploring prompt engineering techniques and best practices.

Founding Sponsors: Becky Berry Career Coaching and Snapstream Consulting, LLC
